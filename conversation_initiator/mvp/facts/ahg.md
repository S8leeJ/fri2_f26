## The building

- This is the Anna Hiss Gymnasium, called AHG, at 2501 Wichita Street on the UT Austin campus.
- AHG is the main home of Texas Robotics.
- It is a historic gym. It was renovated for robotics and reopened in 2021.
- It has about 55,000 square feet: open North and South wings, enclosed labs, and four fabrication shops.
- It has a 1,200 square foot motion capture studio.
- It has a simulated apartment for service robots and a simulated operating room for surgical robots.
- It has a Heavy Robotics Bay for robots that work in hazardous places.
- Texas Robotics brings together Computer Science, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Aerospace Engineering.

## Labs and who leads them

Texas Robotics research groups. Many of them work in AHG.

- Living with Robots Lab, Dr. Justin Hart: service robots for homes, workplaces and public spaces. Human-robot interaction, semantic mapping, and robots that use large language models.
- Learning Agents Research Group, Dr. Peter Stone: machine learning, especially reinforcement learning, and multi-robot systems.
- Autonomous Mobile Robotics Lab, Dr. Joydeep Biswas: robots that stay autonomous and robust in the real world.
- Robot Perception and Learning Lab, Dr. Yuke Zhu: general-purpose robots that reason about and act in the real world.
- Robotic Interactive Intelligence Lab, Dr. Roberto Martin-Martin: robot learning, perception and manipulation, for robotic helpers.
- Human Centered Robotics Lab, Dr. Luis Sentis: humanoid robots and walking on two legs.
- Nuclear and Applied Robotics Group, Dr. Mitch Pryor: robots for hazardous environments.
- ReNeu Robotics Lab, Dr. Ashish Deshpande: robots for rehabilitation and better prostheses.
- HeRo Lab, Dr. Ann Majewicz Fey: robots that work with people in surgery and medical training.
- Augmenting Human Mechanics Lab, Dr. Nick Fey: wearable assistive technology.
- Control and Learning for Autonomous Robotics Lab, Dr. David Fridovich-Keil: control theory, machine learning and game theory.
- Robotics, Sensing and Networks Lab, Dr. Volkan Isler: teams of robots that sense and communicate.
- MERGe Lab, Dr. Lillian Chin: robot materials shaped to fit the robot's task.
- SWARM Lab, Dr. Sandeep Chinchali: fleets of networked robots and drones.
- Clinical Neuroprosthetics and Brain Interaction Lab, Dr. José del R. Millán: using brain signals to control robots and prostheses.
- Center for Autonomy, Dr. Ufuk Topcu: design and verification of autonomous systems.
- ARTS Lab: flexible and soft robots for less invasive surgery.

Dr. José del R. Millán is the Director of Texas Robotics. Sridevi Rao is the Managing Director.
